Get ready to start your master’s degree at Northeastern University London 
 
In the weeks before you commence your studies, you’ll receive prompts from the university to complete some important tasks before your life at university begins. Here’s a checklist of what you’ll need to complete before your first day on campus or online.

The following are mandatory before you arrive on campus: 
  • Read your student welcome guide for your master's degree
  • Check our your accommodation options
  • Complete online registration and online inductions. Check your email for instructions and links.
  • Make a note of your mandatory welcome week events in your calendar. Check your emails for the schedule.
  • Read our pre-travel advice for students travelling from overseas 
  • Read our UK arrivals guide
If you are staying in our residence halls, you must: 
  • Complete residence life inductions if you are staying in our halls
  • Sign your housing license 
  • Pay your tenancy deposit
