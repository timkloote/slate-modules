Enrollment Agreement Required

Your campus location has a regulatory requirement to review and sign a document that is referred to as an "Enrollment Agreement," which provides an overview of your academic information such as program, credits, estimated costs, and other key information. It is not a tuition bill or balance sheet. This is a mandatory requirement for your jurisdiction and cannot be waived. 

In the coming weeks, you will receive an email from adobesign@adobesign.com which enables you to access, review, sign, and submit the enrollment agreement. The signed enrollment agreement is then saved within Northeastern University's system. Enrollment agreements need to be signed prior to the first day of class.

If you have any question pertaining to your enrollment agreement, please refer to Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ).
