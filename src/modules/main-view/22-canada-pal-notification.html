Please note: beginning Fall 2025, Canadian campuses will require a Provincial Attestation Letter (PAL). You have confirmed your intent to enroll and affirmed that you understand campus preferences of Canada are not guaranteed. Your accepted campus will be confirmed once PAL decisions have been rendered. Applicants that are determined to be PAL eligible will then need to pay a $1,500 CAN deposit to receive the POA/PAL. If you have already previously paid the $500 deposit and did not apply for a visa prior to January 22, 2025, that will be refunded.
