If you plan to study on an F-1 student visa and your enrollment deposit deadline is after May 1, we strongly encourage you to pay your enrollment deposit before that date. Paying your enrollment deposit is the first step in requesting an I-20, and submitting your I-20 request as early as possible ensures ample time for visa processing and helps prevent delays. Learn more about requesting your I-20 here.

Also please visit this website for new student webinar information, including past event recording. These webinars will review how to request a Form I-20 and apply for an F-1 visa, living off campus as an international student, and resources for international students studying in the U.S.
