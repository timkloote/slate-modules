Apply to Multiple Programs!

At Northeastern, we recognize that your academic and career interests can often span multiple disciplines. As such, many successful applicants apply to 1-3 programs that align with their diverse interests. Simply use the Submit Request link above and complete the Add Program(s) Request form to apply to one or more programs that match your goals.

Not sure which programs to choose? Visit our graduate programs website and navigate to your primary program's information page. Scroll to the RELATED PROGRAMS - Explore related programs section, where you'll find up to 3 suggested programs.

Applying to multiple programs increases your chances of finding the perfect fit for your academic journey!
