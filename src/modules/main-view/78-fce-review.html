Important Notification

A final review of your academic credentials needs to be completed by Northeastern University.

For U.S.-Bound Students

Submitting your deposit will initiate this final review process. You will receive an email notification regarding your status in this process within ten (10) business days of submitting your deposit.

If you are an international student, please note that you should not initiate the application for the Form I-20 (F-1 – U.S. only) or Form DS-2019 (J-1 - U.S. only) until this final review is completed.

For Canada-Bound Students:

Your Foreign Credential Evaluation must be completed to receive a PAL/POA. Please refer to your Foreign Evaluation checklist for more details on the status of this process.

You may have a registration hold until this process is completed.
