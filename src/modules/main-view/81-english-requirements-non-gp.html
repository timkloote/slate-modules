You must meet the minimum English language proficiency required. Additionally, your offer of admission is contingent upon your satisfaction of all local, state, and federal laws.

The English Language proficiency requirement can be found at: https://cps.northeastern.edu/admissions-aid/international-admissions/applications/english-language-requirements/. Once you have submitted proof that you have met the required English language proficiency scores, you will then be eligible to receive an I-20.

There are several programs available to help you improve your English Language skills. As a convenience to our students we have an established partnership with the Global Pathways program. If you are interested in more information about Global Pathways please visit https://international.northeastern.edu/global-pathways/ or send an email to globalpathways@northeastern.edu.

Upon successfully meeting the condition of your acceptance, you will be formally accepted into the program and admitted to the University. If the condition is not met, your conditional admittance will be rescinded and you will not be able to continue to register for classes. You will be notified should any changes to your condition of acceptance and/or program requirements occur and be held to the new standards. It is also important to note that you are required to follow the program curriculum that is in effect during the term of your first enrollment. Please review the curricula at the beginning of your start term to be sure that you have the most up to date information.
