Northeastern collects Social Security Numbers to support the distribution of required tax documents to all US Citizens or permanent resides, and to be considered for need -based financial aid using the FAFSA.

Social Security Number is required if (i) you are a U.S. Citizen or permanent resident, or (ii) you are a non-U.S. students but would like to receive a 1098-T form.  We will handle and protect your information in accordance with the applicable Northeastern Privacy Policy.
