Conditional Admit Notice

Students are conditionally admitted until their official admissions file is complete. Students must also satisfy any additional provisions or conditions of their acceptance if stated on the official decision letter or college admissions website. Please arrange to complete your official admissions file and meet any other additional requirements prior to enrolling at Northeastern University.
