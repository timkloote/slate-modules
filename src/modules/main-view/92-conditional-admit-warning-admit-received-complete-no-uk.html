Conditional Offer Notice

You have received a conditional offer of admission, which means your place is subject to the completion of your admissions file and the fulfilment of any specific conditions outlined in your official offer letter or on the university’s admissions webpage. Please ensure that you meet all conditions and submit any required documentation prior to enrolling at Northeastern University London.

A key part of completing your admissions file is providing official confirmation of the award of your undergraduate degree. If you are currently completing your undergraduate studies, you may wait to send your official transcript until your degree has been awarded. Once you have graduated, please submit official documentation confirming the conferral of your degree, including the date of award.

If you have already arranged for the necessary documents to be submitted but your application checklist remains incomplete, please be aware that it may take several weeks for your status to be updated. Once all official documents have been received, we will review your file to confirm that all conditions have been met and will contact you if anything further is required.
